Privacy Policy for FestVault

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FestVault ("we", "our", or "the App"), curated and developed by GOPAGANI ARUN, handles user data, permissions, and security.

1. Information We Collect

FestVault is designed as a private, secure celebration photo gallery:

  • Account & Guest Access Information: When guests log in via passcode or credentials, we may collect the guest's name, relation to host, and optional contact number solely for attendee greeting and celebration event management.
  • Photo Uploads & Media: Photos uploaded by authorized hosts and attendees are stored securely within the private archive for viewing and high-resolution downloading by celebration members.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Technical diagnostic details (device screen resolution, operating system version) to provide optimal full-screen rendering.

2. How We Use Information

  • To allow celebration members and invited guests to view and download celebration photos in Full HD.
  • To protect celebration archives against unauthorized public access.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or photos to third parties or advertising brokers.

3. Photos & Media Ownership

All photos displayed within FestVault remain the exclusive property of their respective owners and celebration attendees. Photos are not used for artificial intelligence training or public commercial purposes.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and token authentication to safeguard your celebration memories and personal information.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Policy (Account & Data Deletion)

Users have full control over their personal information and content:

  • You may request deletion of your attendee information, account details, or any uploaded media at any time.
  • To submit a data deletion request, email us directly at gopaganiarungoud@gmail.com with the subject line "FestVault Data Deletion Request". We will process and permanently remove your data within 30 days.
